Add a Resource / Knowledge Center Section

High-end agencies add educational authority content because it does three powerful things:

1️⃣ Boosts SEO dramatically
2️⃣ Makes the organization look like experts
3️⃣ Builds trust with families researching services

This is especially powerful for healthcare, nonprofit, and mental health organizations.

The Section to Add

Place this section between Testimonials and the Final CTA.

Section Title

Helpful Resources for Families and Caregivers

Section Description

Access educational guides, articles, and resources designed to help families better understand intellectual and developmental disability support programs and available community services.

Article Cards Layout

Use 3 article cards.

Why This Makes the Site Look Expensive

This section tells visitors:

• “This organization knows what they're talking about.”
• “They educate the community.”
• “They are a trusted authority.”

Google also loves this content, which helps rank for searches like:

disability support programs

developmental disability services

independent living support

Design Tip (Agency Level)

Make the articles look like modern blog cards:

Include:

• thumbnail image
• title
• short description
• "Read More" link
• subtle hover animation

Add soft shadows and rounded edges.

SEO Benefit

Each article becomes a new ranking opportunity.

For example:

"Understanding Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities"

could rank for thousands of searches.
